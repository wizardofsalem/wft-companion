Before anything is sent

This app sends what the WoW Forever Guild Chronicle addon records in your game to wowforevertracker.com. Your guild's page shows it there.

You choose what is sent. Nothing is sent until you finish here. You can change it later.

What we never collect

  • Chat or whisper text
  • Where other players are
  • Notes or private messages
  • Your Battle.net login or email
  • Anything else on your computer
